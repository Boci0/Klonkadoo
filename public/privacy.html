KLONKADOO: Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 September 2026

This policy explains how the game KLONKADOO (the "App"), published by Boci ("I", "me"), handles information. The short version: the App does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

1. Information the App collects

None. The App has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party tracking SDKs. On Android it uses three permissions: vibration for optional haptic feedback (you can turn it off in Settings), internet only to check for updates (see section 2), and install packages so an update you choose to download can be handed to Android's installer. None of these give the App access to your data.

2. Update checks

The Android and Windows apps check GitHub (github.com, operated by GitHub, Inc.) for a newer version when they start, or when you tap Settings → Updates. This is a plain request for the latest release: no game data, identifiers or personal information are sent. As with any web request, GitHub can see your IP address; see GitHub's own privacy statement. Updates are only downloaded and installed when you tap Update.

3. Information stored on your device

To save your progress, the App stores game data locally on your device only: tech points, upgrades, gear, run statistics, any run in progress (so you can continue after closing the App), the selected risk level and your sound, music and vibration settings. This data never leaves your device through the App.

If you have Android's own device backup turned on, Android may include this game data in your personal Google backup. That backup is controlled by you and Google, and I cannot access it.

4. Clipboard (Export / Import Save)

If you tap Export Save, your save data is copied to your device clipboard so you can store it yourself. If you tap Import Save, the App reads the text you paste. The App only uses the clipboard when you tap these buttons, and never sends that data anywhere.

5. Support page and feedback

The Support page can open your email app or a web browser, and can copy a link to the game to your clipboard. These only happen when you tap them. If you choose to email feedback, I receive your email address and whatever you write, and use them only to reply and to improve the game. I don't share them with anyone, and you can ask me to delete them at any time.

6. Deleting your data

You can delete all game data at any time with Settings → Reset, or by uninstalling the App or clearing its storage in Android settings.

7. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. Because it collects no personal data from anyone, it collects none from children either.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, for example because a future version adds a feature that handles data, the updated policy will be posted at this address with a new effective date before that version is released.
